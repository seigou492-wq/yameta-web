Privacy Policy

Habit Breaker: YAMETA · Updated September 9, 2026 · 日本語

Habit Breaker: YAMETA records the habits you choose to quit and shows elapsed time, estimated money saved, and time reclaimed.

1. No account required

You can use the app without creating an account. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Information stored on your device

Your habit names, start dates, usual spending and time, goals, interests, future-self notes, chosen photos, language preference, birth year, and optional gender are stored locally. The app shares relevant data with its widget on your device. We do not transmit these records to a developer-operated server.

You can delete your habit records and goal photos in the app. Device backups and app removal are managed by your operating system.

3. Purchases

Habit Breaker: YAMETA Plus unlocks tracking for up to 10 habits through a monthly subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple processes payments and purchase restoration through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's processing.

4. Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you for advertising. We do not sell your habit records or share them with third parties.

5. Links and support

If you open a website linked from the app, your browser connects to that website. These public support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting privacy practices apply to website requests. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send so we can answer your request.

6. Policy updates and contact

We will publish policy changes on this page. Contact: seigou492@gmail.com.

本アプリの利用にあたり、アカウント登録やログインは必要ありません。氏名・メールアドレス・電話番号などの個人を特定する情報を取得することはありません。
