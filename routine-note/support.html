Routine Note（ルーティンノート） · 2026-09-08

Routine Note（ルーティンノート）は、予定時刻と制限時間を設定して習慣を管理するアプリです。完了ボタンで達成を記録し、自動記録を有効にしたルーティンは制限時間を過ぎると未完了として表示されます。

English support

Routine Note schedules routines and records completion when you tap Done. With automatic recording enabled, overdue routines are shown as missed. Contact seigou492@gmail.com with the app name, device model, iOS version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.

To restore purchases, use the same Apple Account and choose Restore purchases. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app or buying Lifetime does not cancel an existing subscription. Request refunds through Apple's Report a Problem service. Check notification permissions, Focus settings and routine schedules if reminders do not appear. We cannot recover local records.
