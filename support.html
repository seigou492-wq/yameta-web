How it works

  1. Choose a habit and when you stopped.
  2. Enter your usual spending and time to see estimates grow.
  3. Keep your goals in view and check your progress in the app or widget.

Free and Plus

Your first habit is free. Habit Breaker: YAMETA Plus unlocks up to 10 habits, with a monthly subscription or one-time purchase. Available prices are shown in the app.

Restore a purchase

Open the upgrade screen and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account used for your purchase. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
